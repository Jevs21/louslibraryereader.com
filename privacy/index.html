Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Lou's Library E-Reader collects no personal data, except what you choose to send as feedback. It sends anonymous usage data, which you can turn off.

  • Your books, reading progress, highlights, bookmarks, shelves and reading stats are stored only on your device.
  • The app has no accounts, advertising or tracking.
  • The app does not send your books or reading data to us or to anyone else.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device.

Feedback

If you send a bug report or feature request from the app (Profile → Send Feedback), it sends:

  • What you write, and whether it's a bug report or a feature request.
  • Your email address, only if you enter one. It's used only to reply to you.
  • The app version, iOS version and device model, so problems can be reproduced. You see these before sending.

Feedback is stored on the app's own server and read only by the developer. It isn't shared, sold or used for advertising, and your IP address isn't recorded. To have your feedback deleted, email support.

Anonymous usage data

To learn which features matter and what to improve, the app sends anonymous counts of how it's used:

  • When the app is opened, with whether it's the first open that day, week or month, and how many days ago the app was installed.
  • When a book is added (EPUB or PDF, and whether from Files, another app or the Share Extension), and how long each reading session lasts.
  • When a highlight, note, bookmark or dictionary word is added, a reading goal is set or reached, or data is exported or imported.
  • With each batch: the app version, iOS version, device model, whether the app came from the App Store or TestFlight, and the region in your language settings (not your location).

It never includes your books, their titles or file names, your highlights or notes, or anything you type. Usage data carries no ID of any kind, so events can't be linked to each other, to your device or to you. Your IP address isn't recorded. To check that requests come from the real app, the server keeps a key for each install, which is stored apart from usage data and never linked to it.

Usage data is stored on the app's own server, read only by the developer, and kept indefinitely. It isn't shared, sold or used for advertising. Because it can't be traced to you, it can't be looked up or deleted for one person.

To stop sending it, turn off Share Anonymous Usage Data in Profile → Data. Nothing is sent while it's off, and events waiting to be sent are discarded.

Louie's pet count

Tapping Louie's photo on the About page adds one to a shared count of pets. Only the total is stored, with nothing about you or your device.

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before that version of the app is released.
