Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Lou's Library E-Reader collects no personal data beyond what's described below.

  • Your books, highlights, notes, bookmarks, shelves and reading progress are stored only on your device. The app never sends them to us or to anyone else.
  • The app has no accounts, advertising or tracking.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device.

Feedback

If you send a bug report or feature request from the app, it sends what you write, your email address if you enter one (used only to reply to you), and the app version, iOS version and device model. To have your feedback deleted, email support.

Anonymous usage data

To learn what to improve, the app sends anonymous usage data, such as which features are used, how long reading sessions last and how often the app is opened, along with the app version, iOS version, device model and region setting. It never includes your books, highlights, notes or anything you type. It carries no ID, so it can't be traced to you, or looked up or deleted for one person.

To stop sending it, turn off Share Anonymous Usage Data in Profile → Data.

Louie's pet count

Tapping Louie's photo on the About page adds one to a shared count of pets. Only the total is stored.

App verification

So that only the real app can send data, the app uses Apple's App Attest, and the server keeps a random ID for each install. It's never stored with your feedback or usage data, and it can't be traced to you, or looked up or deleted for one person.

How data is stored

Data the app sends is stored on the app's own server, which it reaches through Cloudflare's network, and may be kept indefinitely. It's used only by the developer, and isn't shared, sold or used for advertising. The app's server doesn't record your IP address.

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before that version of the app is released.
